# Category Playbooks

Select 6-10 modules. Every factual claim must map to a `user` or `visible` fact in `page-spec.json`.

## 3C and Consumer Electronics

Recommended order:

1. Hero with product form and primary verified purpose.
2. Main function or interaction.
3. Controls, ports, or structural detail.
4. Usage environment.
5. Compatibility only when supplied.
6. Dimensions and weight only when supplied.
7. Package contents only when supplied.
8. Instructions, charging, or care only when supplied.
9. Closing summary.

Use precise geometry, cool or neutral lighting, material separation, and diagram-like spacing. Never invent battery life, power, wireless protocol, waterproofing, charging speed, certifications, or device compatibility.

## Beauty and Personal Care

Recommended order:

1. Sensory hero.
2. Intended routine or application area.
3. Texture and packaging detail.
4. Ingredient story only from supplied ingredients.
5. Application steps.
6. Suitable audience only when verified.
7. Size and package information.
8. Cautions or storage.
9. Closing ritual.

Prefer clean tactile imagery, skin-safe visual language, and calm copy. Never create medical, therapeutic, whitening, anti-aging, hypoallergenic, pregnancy-safe, or dermatologist-tested claims without evidence. Do not fabricate before/after results.

## Apparel and Accessories

Recommended order:

1. Silhouette and styling hero.
2. Fit and intended look.
3. Fabric appearance and construction details.
4. Styling context.
5. Movement or carry/use demonstration.
6. Size chart from supplied measurements.
7. Color or variant overview.
8. Care instructions.
9. Closing outfit or detail.

Preserve cut, seams, hardware, pattern placement, and color. Do not infer fiber composition, stretch, waterproofing, warmth rating, or measurements.

## Home, Kitchen, and Organization

Recommended order:

1. Product in a relevant space.
2. Problem and visible outcome.
3. Key mechanism or structure.
4. Capacity or dimensions only when supplied.
5. Before/after only when the same real setup is documented.
6. Installation or use steps.
7. Cleaning and storage.
8. Package contents.
9. Closing organized scene.

Use credible domestic scale and natural light. Do not generate impossible capacity, load, heat resistance, food-contact safety, or mounting strength.

## Food and Beverage

Recommended order:

1. Packaging and serving hero.
2. Flavor or sensory language only from supplied information.
3. Ingredient and origin facts.
4. Preparation or serving steps.
5. Net content and pack count.
6. Nutrition and allergen information from the label.
7. Storage and shelf life from supplied packaging.
8. Occasion or pairing.
9. Closing pack shot.

Use actual packaging and food appearance. Never invent ingredients, nutrition, origin, organic status, health effects, allergens, shelf life, or certifications.

## Pet Products

Recommended order:

1. Product and intended animal context.
2. Primary use.
3. Material/structure appearance.
4. Scale with the intended animal only when size suitability is known.
5. Use steps.
6. Cleaning.
7. Dimensions and package.
8. Safety notes.
9. Closing scene.

Do not claim indestructibility, veterinary approval, anxiety relief, medical benefit, breed/weight suitability, or non-toxicity without evidence.

## Handmade, Gifts, and Collectibles

Recommended order:

1. Original product photo.
2. Material and finish detail.
3. Scale.
4. Process evidence.
5. Personalization options.
6. Use or display setting.
7. Variations.
8. Packaging and gifting.
9. Care.

For Etsy, keep actual seller photography as the product evidence. Do not use synthetic renderings as upload-ready listing photos.

## Regulated and High-Risk Categories

Treat medical, supplement, cosmetic efficacy, children's safety, battery, protective equipment, automotive safety, and ingestible products as high risk.

Before generating:

- identify the exact target market;
- request the single most consequential missing compliance fact;
- verify current platform category rules;
- exclude claims not backed by supplied documents;
- add a seller-review warning to the manifest.
