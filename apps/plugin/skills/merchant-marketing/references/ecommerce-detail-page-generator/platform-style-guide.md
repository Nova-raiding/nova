# Platform Style Guide

Use this file for visual and conversion strategy. Hard limits live in `platform-profiles.json`; do not turn the observations below into compliance claims.

## Shared Principle

Build the visual system from the product fingerprint first:

- quiet, refined products need controlled whitespace and restrained proof;
- playful, low-ticket products can use faster rhythm and brighter contrast;
- technical products need diagrams, hierarchy, and specification clarity;
- tactile or handmade products need material truth, scale, process, and human context.

Platform styling changes the sales grammar, not the identity of the product.

## 淘宝 / 天猫

- **Conversion model:** immersive mobile scroll that moves from desire to proof to reassurance.
- **Visual rhythm:** cinematic opener, alternating full-bleed scenes and structured proof panels.
- **Typography:** strong Chinese display headline, short support copy, clear spec hierarchy.
- **Aesthetic:** brand-led and editorial for Tmall; more flexible and promotional for Taobao.
- **Prioritize:** product mood, differentiating design, close detail, use context, specs, care, brand close.
- **Avoid:** every module using the same centered-product composition; excessive decorative effects that obscure proof.

## 京东

- **Conversion model:** rational trust, technical clarity, fulfillment confidence.
- **Visual rhythm:** denser than Tmall, with faster access to parameters and function evidence.
- **Typography:** compact headline system, labels, numbers, tables, structured callouts.
- **Aesthetic:** clean, precise, engineered, dependable.
- **Prioritize:** feature mechanism, dimensions, compatibility, package contents, materials only when verified.
- **Avoid:** vague luxury language without measurable or visible proof.

## 拼多多

- **Conversion model:** immediate comprehension and obvious value.
- **Visual rhythm:** fast, high-contrast, one idea per screen.
- **Typography:** large mobile-first headlines and short proof points.
- **Aesthetic:** energetic and direct, but never imitate platform badges or invent discounts.
- **Prioritize:** practical outcome, quantity/package facts, ease of use, size, visible comparison.
- **Avoid:** fake price anchors, fake urgency, fake subsidies, unreadable “poster wall” layouts.

## 抖音电商

- **Conversion model:** demonstration and immediate visual payoff.
- **Visual rhythm:** hook, action, result, proof, then concise specification.
- **Typography:** short, conversational, video-caption-like hierarchy in detail modules only.
- **Aesthetic:** dynamic framing, close interaction, strong before-action/after-action sequence only when the outcome is factual.
- **Prioritize:** how it works, use gesture, speed/ease supported by facts, real-world context.
- **Avoid:** static catalog repetition and claims that cannot be demonstrated.

## 小红书电商

- **Conversion model:** taste alignment, credible recommendation, lifestyle fit.
- **Visual rhythm:** editorial cover, airy lifestyle, tactile details, routine, honest specification.
- **Typography:** low-density, magazine-like, soft hierarchy with generous margins.
- **Aesthetic:** natural light, lived-in environments, coherent palette, restrained graphic elements.
- **Prioritize:** why it fits a lifestyle, texture, color, routine, styling, real scale.
- **Avoid:** loud discount graphics, over-polished synthetic scenes, dense spec walls near the opening.

## Amazon

- **Conversion model:** searchable benefit clarity followed by A+ proof and comparison-free reassurance.
- **Visual rhythm:** modular, scannable, benefit headline plus evidence.
- **Typography:** concise native-market copy; avoid embedding excessive small text in images because A+ also supports separate text fields.
- **Aesthetic:** clean commercial photography, controlled brand palette, broad accessibility.
- **Prioritize:** primary benefit, feature mechanism, lifestyle use, dimensions, instructions, verified compatibility.
- **Avoid:** unsubstantiated superlatives, competitor references, guarantees, pricing, reviews, or promotional language in A+ assets.

## AliExpress

- **Conversion model:** fast cross-language comprehension and objection handling.
- **Visual rhythm:** clear hero, icons or short labels, detail evidence, dimensions, package, usage.
- **Typography:** simple English or target-language phrases, large enough for mobile.
- **Aesthetic:** brighter and more explicit than Amazon, while retaining visual consistency.
- **Prioritize:** universal visual proof, size, variants, installation, package contents, shipping-safe expectations.
- **Avoid:** idioms, long paragraphs, regionally confusing scenes, unverified compatibility.

## Temu

- **Conversion model:** visual immediacy and practical value.
- **Visual rhythm:** product-first cards, obvious use, quick feature isolation, package and size clarity.
- **Typography:** use only after the current seller backend confirms text is allowed.
- **Aesthetic:** bright, direct, high product visibility, limited decoration.
- **Prioritize:** what it is, how it is used, what is included, visible details.
- **Avoid:** platform-lookalike promotion badges and any unverified price or discount language.

## TikTok Shop

- **Conversion model:** truthful product gallery supporting shoppable content.
- **Visual rhythm:** front physical view, other angles, details, actual use, scale, variants, package.
- **Typography:** no added text in listing images.
- **Aesthetic:** real, sharp, well-lit, uncluttered, consistent.
- **Prioritize:** user's real photos and accurate crops.
- **Avoid:** digital renderings, generated scenes, graphics, watermarks, borders, text, or accessories not included.

## eBay

- **Conversion model:** condition and item certainty.
- **Visual rhythm:** complete visual inspection rather than an advertising long page.
- **Typography:** keep listing images mostly text-free; put facts in item specifics and description.
- **Aesthetic:** neutral, honest, detailed, consistent angle coverage.
- **Prioritize:** exact item, condition, scale, labels, defects, package contents.
- **Avoid:** hiding wear, aggressive retouching, decorative scenes that obscure condition.

## Etsy

- **Conversion model:** authenticity, craft, maker story, tactile confidence.
- **Visual rhythm:** original hero photo, detail, scale, process, lifestyle, variation, packaging.
- **Typography:** minimal; listing copy carries the story.
- **Aesthetic:** human, tactile, natural, distinctive without looking mass-produced.
- **Prioritize:** seller's original actual-product photos, material appearance, process evidence, realistic scale.
- **Avoid:** generated product renderings, stock-photo look, false handmade signals, or styling that changes the actual item.

## Shopify

- **Conversion model:** owned-brand narrative with full control over page sections.
- **Visual rhythm:** editorial hero, benefits, feature storytelling, lifestyle, proof, specs, FAQ, close.
- **Typography:** follow supplied brand system; otherwise create a restrained two-level display/body pairing.
- **Aesthetic:** category and brand specific; maximize whitespace and consistency rather than marketplace density.
- **Prioritize:** brand differentiation, product story, conversion objections, responsive negative space.
- **Avoid:** copying marketplace layouts into a D2C site or using one generic aesthetic across categories.
